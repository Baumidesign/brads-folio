### Overview

Charnwood South Africa imports British-made wood-burning stoves and sells them through stockists across South Africa and Namibia. In 2015 I rebuilt their website on WordPress, replacing an older site.

### A long-term client

After launch I stayed on for about seven years, until around 2022. I hosted the site, ran monthly maintenance and updates, and did ongoing SEO work to help them show up when South Africans searched for stoves.

### Where it is now

The site is still live today, and still runs on the layout and structure I first built.
