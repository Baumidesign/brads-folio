### Overview

Atlantic Letting has let luxury holiday homes in Cape Town since 2003. Their guests are visitors looking for a villa or apartment near the city's beaches and suburbs.

Around 2019 I designed their website and built it on WordPress with a custom theme I wrote, rather than an off-the-shelf one. Guests can search properties by suburb, price and number of bedrooms, browse each suburb's homes, and find tours and extras like private chefs and wine tours.

### After launch

I stayed on to run monthly maintenance and updates, keeping the site secure and working smoothly.

### Where it is now

The site is still live today, and still runs on the design I built.
