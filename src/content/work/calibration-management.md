### Overview

Calibration Management calibrates equipment for businesses across Australia, from medical and food to electrical, pressure, torque and metrology. In 2017 I redesigned their website and rebuilt it on WordPress with a custom theme I wrote, so customers can see what the lab calibrates and request a quote.

### Looking after it

Since launch I've hosted the site, run monthly maintenance and updates, and worked on SEO so the lab shows up when businesses search for calibration services.

### Where it is now

The site is still live today, and still runs on the design I built.
